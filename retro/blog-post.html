MOST AI DATA PROJECTS FAIL AT STEP TWO

SAUZI TEAM Implementation notes from the last six builds

Teams connect the pipelines, stand up the warehouse, point an AI tool at it, and get confident wrong answers. The tooling worked. The data was there. The answers were still wrong.

The missing step is structuring: building the dimension and fact tables an agent needs to reason about the business. Most implementations skip it, and fail because of it.

THE SHORT VERSION

AN AGENT CAN ONLY ANSWER QUESTIONS YOUR SCHEMA CAN EXPRESS.

WHAT STEP TWO ACTUALLY IS

Phase 1 gets clean data flowing into a warehouse. Phase 3 connects the AI tools. Between them sits the work nobody demos: deciding what a customer is, what an order is, which of the four revenue numbers is the revenue number, and writing that down in tables an agent has to query through.

Skip it and the agent improvises. It joins the wrong tables, counts a refund twice, treats a subscription renewal as a new customer. It answers instantly and it answers wrong, which is worse than a slow analyst.

HOW TO TELL IF YOU SKIPPED IT

Three questions. Ask the same one twice, in two different sessions, and see whether the number moves. Ask for repeat purchase rate and check whether it matches what your email platform reports. Ask which customers are at risk of churning and see whether the agent explains how it decided.

If the answers drift, the structure isn't there. That's a modeling problem, not a prompt problem, and no amount of tool switching fixes it.
