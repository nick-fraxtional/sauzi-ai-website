# Sauzi.ai Brand Assets v0.2

This package contains the approved concept-stage identity system for sauzi.ai.

## Identity roles

- **Data Rally** is the permanent corporate mark and default logo.
- **Data Sentinel** is the guardian identity for infrastructure, security, governance, reliability, and enterprise delivery.
- **Query Ranger** is the explorer identity for analytics, discovery, AI agents, product guidance, and experimentation.
- **Stack Knight** is the builder identity for implementation, integrations, modernization, deployment, and assembling reliable data stacks.

## Package contents

- `assets/data-rally/`: transparent PNG mark and horizontal lockup.
- `assets/data-sentinel/`: transparent PNG emblem, full-body character, horizontal lockup, and app icon.
- `assets/query-ranger/`: transparent PNG emblem, full-body character, horizontal lockup, and app icon.
- `assets/stack-knight/`: transparent PNG emblem, full-body character, horizontal lockup, and app icon.
- `concept-boards/`: the four approved presentation boards.
- `brand-guide/`: the shareable PDF guide.

## Palette

- Near black: `#191816`
- Warm cream: `#F1EDE4`
- Signal amber: `#F4A300`
- Burnt orange: `#B6542E`

## Status

These are AI-generated raster concepts intended for review, prototyping, internal presentations, and early digital exploration. Production release should follow a vector-redraw pass, small-size testing, spacing refinement, and trademark review.

## Core usage rule

Use Data Rally when clarity and corporate recognition matter most. Use one mascot at a time according to the story being told; do not combine multiple mascots into one compact logo.
